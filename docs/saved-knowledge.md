# Save a response to knowledge

On iPhone, tap the bookmark beside a completed assistant response, or long-press and choose **Save to knowledge**. On Mac, right-click the response and choose the same action. A filled bookmark indicates a saved response. Repeat the action to remove it from saved knowledge.

The existing knowledge base automatically extracts notes and organizes concepts such as business definitions, table grain, columns, and join rules. Facts and decisions persist regardless of source; only temporary progress notes expire after 14 days. Explicit saves retain the response as a durable reference, keeping its source conversation, timestamp, and assistant-reported provenance. Saving is an importance signal, not verification.

Saved text becomes searchable immediately through memory and Knowledge; the background editor incorporates the evidence into canonical linked articles. Existing topic assignments are reused initially; otherwise a temporary Saved evidence topic is created. The editor routes that evidence into the appropriate wiki subjects. Retrieval is shared across projects and remains scoped to relevant searches; every saved response is not inserted into every prompt.

Complete response text is retained in bounded evidence chunks, with recognized credentials redacted. Images, attached files, and linked documents are not copied by this action. Unsave removes explicitly saved chunks while retaining the conversation and any independently extracted notes. Forgetting a source or deleting its conversation also removes its saved knowledge.
